Problem Statement
As Aarav looked around he noticed that everything seemed to be made up of basic shapes. The table was a rectangle, the clock a circle, and even the windows were squares. Aarav was curious why are things made of shapes .Let us help Aarav understand drawing with shapes.

Description:
This project aims to familiarise you to the concept of drawing with shapes. We will use various shapes to create an interesting sketch and fill colours in it.

Additional Requirement
PC or Laptop

Problem Statement
Joanna and her mom went shopping and made bill payments using a cell phone. Joanna asked her mom how she paid her bills without giving cash. Her mom helped her understand this method. Can we also assist her?

Description:
The project emphasizes the learning of wireless communication using technologies such as Bluetooth devices, NFC, Infrared and Wi-Fi hotspots for transferring data from one device to another.

Additional Requirement
PC or Laptop

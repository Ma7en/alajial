Problem Statement
Jonath shows his father a photo of himself and his friends that he took on a school trip. While showing him his photo, he worried that it was a group photo of just him and his friends. But, all the photos have people around. Jonath’s father taught him a solution to removing unnecessary things from photos. Can we try that too?

Description:
This project emphasizes the learning of photo retouching in the image editor pixlr which will remove image defects or unwanted elements from the photograph to make it clear.

Additional Requirement
PC or Laptop

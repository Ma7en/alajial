Problem Statement
Lila is planning a picnic with her friends. She repeated everything she needed for the picnic over and over again so she wouldn't forget anything. Her mother noticed this and suggested a new computer tool that could neatly arrange and organize her data. Can we also learn about it

Description:
This project emphasizes learning about data, information, tables, format, and Excel spreadsheets, as well as creating a spreadsheet of vegetable lists using the Excel software.

Problem Statement
Sarah loves drawing on her laptop but she is stuck. She finds it difficult to show the Sun hiding behind mountains. That's when her mother tells her about 'layers' - a cool trick in drawing. Sarah is excited to try. Let us help Sarah in creating this digital drawing.

Description:
This project aims to familiarise you with the concept of layers in digital drawing. We will draw different objects on different layers and form one picture.

Additional Requirement
PC or Laptop

Problem Statement
Diya is wearing a fitness band. The feature in the fitness band indicates her body temperature when she presses it. Diya's sister enquired wondering how it was happening. Can we give her an explanation

Description:
This project emphasizes the learning of Sensors and their applications and to create an automatic light control circuit to understand the workings of a sensor.

Additional Requirement
Broadband. LDR SENSOR Light Dependent Sensor. LED. Resistor 10 k ohms. 9V Battery. Jumber Wires

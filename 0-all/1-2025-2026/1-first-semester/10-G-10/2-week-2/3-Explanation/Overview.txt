Problem Statement
Alex received a large wholesale order from a new client, but he struggled to quickly locate specific product details and pricing. Additionally, he spent too much time manually updating inventory and couldnt easily identify which products were running low. Can we find him a solution to streamline his inventory management and improve efficiency?

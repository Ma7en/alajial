Problem Statement
Pedro an animal lover noticed many stray dogs in his community. Concerned about their well-being he decided to create a website to connect with people. Driven by his passion for animal welfare Pedro started learning web development. Can we assist Pedro in creating an effective website to promote animal welfare.

Description:
This project aims to develop a dynamic and user-friendly animal welfare website using Node.js, a popular runtime environment for building server-side applications. This website will serve as a comprehensive platform dedicated to improving the lives of animals by addressing various challenges related to their well-being, care, and protection.

Additional Requirement
PC or Laptop

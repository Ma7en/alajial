Problem Statement
Samuel has participated in a science project exhibition. He needs to show a physical as well as a digital model of his exhibit. He has created a 3D model of his project; however, he is unsure how he can show a working model of the project. Can you help him to animate his project so he can showcase it digitally as well?

Description:
This project aims to visualize 3D shapes and create a 3D model of a pendulum and animate it using the animation techniques in Blender.

Additional Requirement
PC or Laptop

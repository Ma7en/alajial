Problem Statement
Ishan's father is watching a rocket launch video on Facebook. Ishan is always eager to learn about the digital world and remembered that he is going to take part in a debate competition at school on the topic of the digital universe. Can we also help him to win the competition?

Description:
The project emphasizes the learning of technology development, the digital world we are living and the advantages and disadvantages of it.

Additional Requirement
PC or Laptop
